Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address and password when you register, and the expense details you log, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect any information you do not choose to enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to generate summaries and filters of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and is protected with reasonable technical safeguards, including hashed passwords. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may request deletion of your account and expense history at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them. Apart from this, we do not share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to access, correct or delete your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
